Calculation and guardrails

Fixed-rate installment formula

Monthly rate r = annual nominal rate / 12; number of remaining months n; principal P. For r > 0, payment = P × r / (1 − (1+r)−n). At zero rate, payment = P/n.

Revised principal = original principal − immediate prepayment Revised term = original months + extension Total scenario outflow = revised installment × revised term + prepayment

These results are illustrative, not a verified contractual schedule.

Controls and privacy

No imported customer data, external packages or outbound requests. Names and amounts are fictitious. Scenarios and draft context are stored only in your browser's local storage; clearing browser site data resets them. All results are generated client-side.
